## 收入——奖励积分
- 奖励积分应分摊货款

$$ \frac{总积分×预计使用率}{货款+(总积分×预计使用率)}×货款=奖励积分应分摊货款 $$

- 积分累计应确认收入

$$ \frac{累计使用积分}{总积分×预计使用率}×积分应分摊货款=积分累计应确认收入 $$

- 例
> **货款=4500，积分=625，预计使用率=0.8**<br>
积分应分摊货款=(625×0.8)/(4500+(625×0.8))×4500=450<br>
第一年实际使用积分400，且预计使用率仍为0.8<br>
第一年累计应确认收入=(400/(625×0.8))×450=360<br>
第一年一年应确认收入=本年-上年=360-0=360<br>

> 第二年实际使用积分80，**但预计使用率変为0.9**<br>
第二年累计应确认收入=((400+80)/(625×0.9))×450=384<br>
第二年一年应确认收入=本年-上年=384-360=24<br>

> **若预计使用率仍为0.8**，则<br>
第二年累计应确认收入=((400+80)/(625×0.8))×450=432<br>
第二年一年应确认收入=本年-上年=432-384=48<br>

## Revenue – Bonus Points *(Eng.)*

- Bonus points should be allocated to the transaction price

$$ \frac{Total\ points \times Estimated\ redemption\ rate}{Consideration + (Total\ points \times Estimated\ redemption\ rate)} \times Consideration = Amount\ allocated\ to\ bonus\ points $$

- Cumulative revenue to be recognized from points

$$ \frac{Cumulative\ points\ redeemed}{Total\ points \times Estimated\ redemption\ rate} \times Amount\ allocated\ to\ bonus\ points = Cumulative\ revenue\ recognized $$

- Example
> Consideration = 4,500, Points = 625, Estimated redemption rate = 0.8  
> Amount allocated to bonus points = (625 × 0.8) / (4,500 + (625 × 0.8)) × 4,500 = 450  
> Year 1: actual points redeemed = 400, estimated redemption rate remains 0.8  
> Cumulative revenue to be recognized in Year 1 = (400 / (625 × 0.8)) × 450 = 360  
> Revenue recognized in Year 1 = current year – prior year = 360 – 0 = 360  

> Year 2: actual points redeemed = 80, **but estimated redemption rate changes to 0.9**  
> Cumulative revenue to be recognized in Year 2 = ((400 + 80) / (625 × 0.9)) × 450 = 384  
> Revenue recognized in Year 2 = current year – prior year = 384 – 360 = 24  

> **If the estimated redemption rate remained 0.8**, then:  
> Cumulative revenue to be recognized in Year 2 = ((400 + 80) / (625 × 0.8)) × 450 = 432  
> Revenue recognized in Year 2 = current year – prior year = 432 – 384 = 48
